Stream Spike Clipper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Stream Spike Clipper handles information when you run the software.

Information used

The software may use Twitch channel names, public Twitch chat messages, stream video data, and authorization tokens for connected Twitch, TikTok, or Instagram accounts.

How information is used

Information is used to detect chat activity, create clips, and publish clips to the social accounts that you explicitly configure. Video recordings and generated clips are stored locally on the computer running the software unless you configure a platform upload.

Sharing

The software sends content only to the platforms you choose to connect. It does not sell personal information.

Security

Keep your environment file and access tokens private. Anyone with access to those credentials may be able to access connected accounts.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings and clips remain on your computer until you delete them. You may revoke platform permissions and remove local files at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the software owner through the account or website where you obtained this application.
